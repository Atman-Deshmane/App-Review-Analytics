## Weekly App Review Pulse: Groww Leadership Briefing
**Reporting Period:** 24 Aug 2026 to 06 Sep 2026

### Executive Summary
This reporting period highlights significant user frustration with **App Navigation Issues**, indicating potential disruptions to trading workflows. While **Trading Features** remain a strong focus of user feedback, the sheer volume and high impact of navigation complaints suggest an immediate need for review and potential rollback or rapid fix of recent changes.

### 1. Top Themes & Strategic Insights

*   **App Navigation Issues (Impact Score: 510, Review Count: 51):** This theme is the dominant concern this period, with a substantial impact score and review count. Users are experiencing critical usability problems, particularly with chart back buttons disappearing and the auto-filling of limit orders. This directly impedes trading efficiency and creates significant user annoyance, demanding immediate attention.
*   **Trading Features (Impact Score: 462, Review Count: 37):** Despite the navigation issues, user requests for enhanced trading features, such as automatic Trailing Stop Loss and specific chart indicators, remain high. The detailed feedback, including competitor comparisons (Fyers), suggests a strong desire for advanced functionalities that could improve trading strategies and outcomes.
*   **Other (Impact Score: 238, Review Count: 184):** While "Other" has the highest review count, its impact score is considerably lower, suggesting a wider range of less critical issues. However, the specific mention of unsolicited account creation (F&O, Bonds) without user consent is a significant concern regarding user trust and data privacy, which could escalate if not addressed.

### 3. Recommended Actions

*   **Immediate investigation and potential rollback/hotfix for App Navigation Issues:** Prioritize resolving the disappearing back button on charts and the auto-filling of limit orders to restore smooth trading functionality and user confidence.
*   **Develop a roadmap for requested Trading Features:** Formulate a plan to address key user requests for advanced trading tools, including Trailing Stop Loss and specific charting indicators, potentially benchmarking against competitor offerings.
*   **Review and revise account creation workflows:** Ensure all account creation processes, especially for F&O and Bonds, are explicitly opt-in and require clear user consent to prevent misleading experiences and maintain user trust.
