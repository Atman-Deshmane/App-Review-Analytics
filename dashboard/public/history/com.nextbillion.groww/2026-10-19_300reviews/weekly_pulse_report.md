## Weekly App Review Pulse: Groww Leadership Briefing
**Reporting Period:** 05 Oct 2026 to 18 Oct 2026

### Executive Summary
App performance continues to be the dominant theme, with strong positive sentiment around recent updates and a clear user request for enhanced P/L visibility. While overall review volume is moderate, a concerning negative quote highlights persistent issues with customer support.

### 1. Top Themes & Strategic Insights

*   **App Performance (Impact Score: 569, Reviews: 53):** This theme demonstrates the highest user engagement and impact. The overwhelmingly positive feedback highlights user appreciation for ongoing development and updates. The top quote provides a specific, actionable suggestion for improving the returns display, which, if implemented, could significantly enhance user experience and reduce friction for a large segment of users.
*   **Other (Impact Score: 174, Reviews: 204):** While this theme has the highest number of reviews, its lower impact score suggests a wider distribution of less critical feedback, including a highly negative sentiment. The presence of extremely negative, albeit generic, feedback in this category warrants further investigation into the underlying causes.
*   **Charges & Fees (Impact Score: 38, Reviews: 15):** Although receiving a lower volume of reviews, the sentiment here is notably negative. The top quote reflects user concern about the affordability of taxes and charges post-budget, indicating potential price sensitivity and a need for clear communication or reassessment of fee structures.

### 3. Recommended Actions

*   **Prioritize P/L Display Enhancement:** Evaluate and fast-track the implementation of displaying daily P/L figures directly within the returns column on the app, as suggested by a highly voted user.
*   **Investigate and Address Customer Support Deficiencies:** Conduct an urgent deep dive into the recurring customer support complaints, particularly those related to data fetching and unresponsiveness, to identify root causes and implement immediate remedial actions.
