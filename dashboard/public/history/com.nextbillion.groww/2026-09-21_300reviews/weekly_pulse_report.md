## Weekly App Review Pulse: Groww Leadership Briefing
**Reporting Period:** 07 Sep 2026 to 20 Sep 2026

### Executive Summary
This period saw a significant surge in positive sentiment driven by "Other" themes, largely attributed to satisfied long-term users. However, critical feedback regarding "App Performance" and "Customer Support" remains a persistent concern, impacting user experience and potentially hindering growth.

### 1. Top Themes & Strategic Insights

*   **Other (193 reviews, 11037 impact score):** The overwhelming impact score in this category highlights a strong base of satisfied users who have been with Groww for a considerable time. This indicates successful long-term customer retention and satisfaction, likely due to the overall value proposition and consistent improvements over time.
*   **App Performance (39 reviews, 98 impact score):** Despite a lower review count, the "App Performance" theme raises a red flag. Reports of chart delays, freezing, and general bugs suggest technical issues that can significantly frustrate users, even if they are generally satisfied with the app's offerings.
*   **Customer Support (17 reviews, 59 impact score):** User dissatisfaction with customer support is evident, with complaints about long wait times and unhelpful agents. This theme, while having fewer reviews, carries a substantial impact score, indicating that when users do require support, their experience is negatively affecting their perception of Groww.

### 2. Voice of the Customer (Quotes)

*   "delay chart, slow chart sometimes stuck. and lots of bugs in this app" - **App Performance:** This quote directly illustrates the critical issues users are facing with the app's responsiveness and stability, impacting core functionality.
*   "Very bad support either it is chat or call. They say wait time is 3 minutes. However no agent for long 20 minutes." - **Customer Support:** This quote highlights a significant pain point in customer service, indicating a disconnect between advertised wait times and actual support availability, leading to frustration.
*   "using it since a year and I can say that it has good interface but it's the highest-charging app. Hidden charges on cancelled/unexecuted orders eat up most of your F&O profit. Withdrawals take 5-7 days and support is unhelpful. Not recommended if you are trading with low profit margin as you will be loser even with profit, grow will eat all your profit through margin and charges." - **Charges and Fees:** This comprehensive quote points to concerns about transparency in charges, the impact of fees on profitability, and slow withdrawal processes, suggesting potential areas for competitive differentiation.

### 3. Recommended Actions

*   **Prioritize App Performance Optimization:** Conduct an immediate deep dive into the technical issues causing chart delays, freezing, and bugs. Allocate engineering resources to resolve these critical performance bottlenecks to improve user experience.
*   **Enhance Customer Support Responsiveness:** Review and optimize customer support staffing levels to meet advertised wait times. Implement efficiency improvements in support processes and provide additional training to agents to ensure more effective issue resolution.
*   **Analyze and Clarify Charges and Fees:** Conduct a thorough review of the fee structure, focusing on transparency and potential impact on user profitability, especially for F&O traders. Consider offering clearer explanations or exploring competitive pricing adjustments.
