## Weekly App Review Pulse: Groww Leadership Briefing
**Reporting Period:** 10 Aug 2026 to 22 Aug 2026

### Executive Summary
This reporting period saw a significant concentration of feedback on **App Performance**, with users highlighting both appreciation for new features and concerns around notification delays. While overall sentiment towards new features like the technical section and "top movers" is positive, the persistent issue of delayed notifications is impacting user experience and potentially trading decisions.

### 1. Top Themes & Strategic Insights

*   **App Performance (Impact Score: 1059, Review Count: 59):** This theme continues to dominate user feedback, indicating its critical importance. Users are actively engaging with and appreciating new additions like the technical section and "your top movers" feature, signaling success in feature development. However, the recurring issue of delayed notifications, particularly for price-sensitive events, is a significant pain point that needs immediate attention to ensure users receive timely information.

*   **Other (Impact Score: 196, Review Count: 184):** While the "Other" category has the highest review count, its low impact score suggests a broad range of less critical or more general comments. The single top quote in this category expresses a strong negative sentiment, potentially indicating a small but vocal group with extreme dissatisfaction. Further segmentation of this category might reveal underlying issues, but for now, it appears less strategically pressing than App Performance.

*   **Features & Tools (Impact Score: 150, Review Count: 28):** Positive sentiment surrounds the improvement of existing features and the introduction of new ones. The specific suggestion for adding "Return Value - Total & Days change" directly reflects a user desire for enhanced portfolio tracking capabilities, mirroring features found on competitor platforms. This indicates an opportunity to further refine the portfolio management tools to meet evolving user expectations.

### 3. Recommended Actions

*   **Prioritize Notification Latency:** Conduct an immediate deep dive into the backend systems responsible for sending real-time market data and notification triggers. Optimize these processes to significantly reduce the 15-minute delay reported by users for critical alerts.
*   **Review Customer Support Escalation & Resolution:** Investigate ticket ID 27281436 and the broader trend of unresolved portfolio data issues and repeated, ineffective support interactions. Implement stricter SLAs for complex issues and ensure support agents are empowered to genuinely resolve problems, not just close tickets.
*   **Enhance Portfolio Analytics:** Evaluate the feasibility of integrating "Return Value - Total & Days change" metrics into the portfolio section. Benchmark against competitor offerings and prioritize this enhancement to meet user demand for more detailed performance tracking.
