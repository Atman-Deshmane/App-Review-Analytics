## Weekly App Review Pulse: Groww Leadership Briefing
**Reporting Period:** 29 Jun 2026 to 12 Jul 2026

### Executive Summary
This period highlights a significant user concern around **App Performance**, driven by negative sentiment and a substantial review volume. While other themes show moderate impact, the recurring issues within trading and customer support warrant immediate attention to mitigate potential churn.

### 1. Top Themes & Strategic Insights

*   **App Performance (Impact Score: 370, Review Count: 229):** This is overwhelmingly the most dominant theme, accounting for the highest review volume and impact score. The prevalence of this theme suggests widespread user dissatisfaction with the app's stability, user experience, or functionality. The top quote, "bekar app," reinforces the negative sentiment. Addressing these underlying performance issues is critical to user retention and overall app perception.

*   **Trading Issues (Impact Score: 59, Review Count: 24):** While lower in volume than App Performance, this theme carries a significant impact, particularly highlighted by a critical quote detailing a substantial financial loss due to an app rejection during a trade. This indicates severe trust erosion and potential financial harm to users, which can lead to immediate churn and reputational damage.

*   **Customer Support (Impact Score: 37, Review Count: 13):** Although the review count is lower, the impact score and the accompanying quote reveal a pattern of perceived incompetence and unresponsiveness from the support team. Users are experiencing incorrect calculations, a lack of follow-up, and the premature closure of support tickets, leading to frustration and a lack of confidence in Groww's ability to resolve issues.

### 3. Recommended Actions

*   **Prioritize App Performance Diagnostics:** Initiate an immediate and in-depth investigation into the root causes of the widespread "App Performance" issues. Focus on app stability, load times, and error resolution, aiming for a demonstrable improvement within the next reporting cycle.

*   **Enhance Trading Execution and Support Protocols:** Conduct a thorough review of the trading engine's reliability, especially concerning order rejections during volatile market conditions. Simultaneously, retrain customer support to handle complex trading issue escalations effectively, ensuring timely and accurate resolutions.

*   **Review Fee Structure Communication and Transparency:** Analyze the impact of the current fee structure on smaller trade volumes. Consider clearer communication of fees or potential adjustments to fee tiers to ensure users, especially those with smaller investment amounts, do not feel penalized or pushed into losses due to transaction costs.
