## Weekly App Review Pulse: Groww Leadership Briefing

**Reporting Period:** 22 Jun 2026 to 04 Jul 2026

### Executive Summary

User feedback this period continues to highlight a strong demand for enhanced trading tools, particularly technical indicators, as seen with the overwhelming interest in the Stochastic indicator. While overall sentiment remains positive, issues related to app performance, specifically chart loading and data limitations, require immediate attention to maintain user satisfaction.

### 1. Top Themes & Strategic Insights

*   **User Interface & Features (Impact Score: 5922, Reviews: 111):** This theme continues to be the dominant driver of user feedback, indicating a strong desire for more advanced trading functionalities. The high impact score suggests that feature requests directly influence user sentiment and engagement.
*   **App Performance & Bugs (Impact Score: 43, Reviews: 30):** While having a lower impact score than UI/Features, the "App Performance & Bugs" theme is critical as it directly affects the core usability of the app. Slow chart loading and limited historical data are hindering effective analysis for users.
*   **Customer Support Issues (Impact Score: 31, Reviews: 12):** This theme, though with fewer reviews, points to a significant pain point. Users are experiencing dissatisfaction with the responsiveness, accuracy, and resolution of support queries, which can lead to frustration and account closure.

*   **App Performance & Bugs:** "chart loading is poor also giving limited number of candle for past data analysis give large number of candles to read past data" (Votes: 3) - This quote directly addresses a technical impediment that impacts the core functionality for active traders.

### 3. Recommended Actions

*   **Prioritize Feature Development:** Expedite the integration of highly requested technical indicators like the Stochastic indicator to enhance trading capabilities and user satisfaction.
*   **Address Performance Bottlenecks:** Investigate and resolve the slow chart loading times and increase the number of available historical data candles to improve the trading analysis experience.
*   **Enhance Customer Support Resolution:** Implement stricter protocols for customer support ticket handling, ensuring accurate initial responses, timely follow-ups, and proper resolution before closing tickets.
