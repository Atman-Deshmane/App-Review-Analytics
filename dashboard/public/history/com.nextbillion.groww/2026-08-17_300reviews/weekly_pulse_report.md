## Weekly App Review Pulse: Groww Leadership Briefing
**Reporting Period:** 03 Aug 2026 to 16 Aug 2026

### Executive Summary
This reporting period saw a significant surge in "App Performance Issues," driven by user-reported lags and delayed notifications, impacting the core user experience. While new features are generally well-received, underlying performance concerns are overshadowing positive feedback.

### 1. Top Themes & Strategic Insights

*   **App Performance Issues (Impact Score: 1303, Review Count: 55):** This theme dominates the review landscape this period, indicating a critical area for immediate attention. The primary driver is user-reported latency in notifications, particularly for market movements and watchlist updates. This suggests that the recent update, while introducing valuable features like the technical section and "top movers," may have introduced performance bottlenecks. The high impact score underscores the negative sentiment generated by these issues.
*   **Data and Features (Impact Score: 164, Review Count: 54):** This theme highlights mixed user sentiment. While new features like the technical section are being praised, there's a growing concern about decreasing transparency in order-related data. This suggests a need to re-evaluate how information is presented to users and ensure that feature enhancements don't come at the cost of clarity.
*   **Customer Support (Impact Score: 110, Review Count: 21):** The reviews in this category point to persistent issues with customer support effectiveness and resolution. Users are reporting unresolved issues, a lack of personalized assistance, and closure of tickets without proper solutions. This directly impacts user trust and satisfaction, especially when combined with technical problems.

*   **"Very Good app. most features we need is all inbuilt. Only thing I found is little lag. most notifications are delayed. From opening bell to my watchlist stocks performance notifications. ### 3. Recommended Actions

*   **Prioritize and Investigate App Performance Bottlenecks:** Dedicate engineering resources to thoroughly diagnose and resolve the reported lags and delayed notifications. Focus on optimizing the real-time data feeds and notification delivery system.
*   **Enhance Customer Support Training and Escalation Protocols:** Implement targeted training for customer support representatives to improve their ability to diagnose and resolve complex technical issues. Streamline escalation processes to ensure that persistent problems are addressed effectively and not closed prematurely.
*   **Review and Communicate Data Transparency Policies:** Conduct an immediate review of order-related data presentation to ensure it meets user expectations for transparency. If changes have been made intentionally, develop clear communication strategies to explain these to users.
