## Weekly App Review Pulse: Groww Leadership Briefing
**Reporting Period:** 27 Jul 2026 to 09 Aug 2026

### Executive Summary
This reporting period highlights significant user dissatisfaction with App Performance, driven by severe glitches leading to substantial financial losses, overshadowing other themes. While User Interface remains a frequent discussion point, the severity of performance issues demands immediate attention to retain user trust and prevent further churn.

### 1. Top Themes & Strategic Insights

*   **App Performance (Impact Score: 327, Reviews: 37):** This theme continues to be the most critical area of concern, with an exceptionally high impact score. The recurring issues of app glitches, manipulation of trading data (buying price reduction), and outright vanishing of funds, as highlighted by a strongly voted quote, are causing severe financial distress and a complete erosion of user trust. The lack of refunds for reported issues exacerbates the problem, leading to app deletions.

*   **User Interface (Impact Score: 188, Reviews: 214):** Despite a lower impact score compared to App Performance, User Interface themes represent the highest volume of reviews. Specific feedback regarding missing OI details for Nifty50 and the absence of PE RATIO details indicates a need for feature parity with other instruments and general usability improvements to enhance the trading experience.

*   **Customer Support (Impact Score: 34, Reviews: 18):** While the review count is relatively low, the sentiment around Customer Support is overwhelmingly negative. Users express extreme dissatisfaction with the current support experience, contrasting it with a better past, and explicitly stating their intention to switch brokers due to poor service. The frustration stems from unhelpful initial responses like re-installing the app, which doesn't resolve underlying issues.

### 3. Recommended Actions

*   **Immediate Root Cause Analysis & Resolution for App Performance Glitches:** Prioritize a comprehensive investigation into the app glitches causing financial losses and data manipulation. Establish a transparent communication channel with affected users, detailing the steps being taken and offering immediate resolution, including refunds where applicable.
*   **Enhance User Interface with Key Data Features:** Conduct a UX review to ensure feature parity for critical trading data (e.g., OI details for all relevant indices, PE RATIO). Expedite the implementation of these requested features to improve usability and user satisfaction.
*   **Revitalize Customer Support Operations:** Implement a robust quality assurance program for customer support interactions. Equip the support team with better tools and training to address technical issues effectively, moving beyond generic troubleshooting steps and demonstrating empathy and a commitment to resolution.
