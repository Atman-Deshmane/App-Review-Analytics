## Weekly App Review Pulse: Groww Leadership Briefing
**Reporting Period:** 09 Nov 2026 to 22 Nov 2026

### Executive Summary
This reporting period highlights a significant concern around "App Performance," with user feedback pointing to server issues and order execution delays leading to potential financial losses. While "Other" themes remain dominant in review volume, the specific impact of performance issues is a critical area requiring immediate attention.

### 1. Top Themes & Strategic Insights

*   **Other (Impact Score: 594, Reviews: 216):** This theme continues to generate the highest volume of reviews and impact score. While broad, a top quote indicates user satisfaction with the core app and UI, but a significant drawback regarding F&O lot purchase limits is highlighted. This suggests a need to delve deeper into the specific "Other" categories to understand the drivers of satisfaction and dissatisfaction beyond core app functionality.

*   **App Performance (Impact Score: 442, Reviews: 45):** This theme, though lower in review count than "Other," has a substantial impact score. The feedback is highly critical, directly linking server problems and order delays to user financial losses and a lack of support. This indicates a critical user experience issue that needs to be prioritized to prevent churn and reputational damage.

*   **Withdrawal Issues (Impact Score: 201, Reviews: 8):** While the number of reviews is low, the "Withdrawal Issues" theme has a concerningly high impact score and a particularly impactful quote. Users are experiencing significant delays in withdrawals, repeated payment method failures, and delayed crediting of funds after selling stocks. This directly impacts user trust and their ability to access their capital.

### 3. Recommended Actions

*   **Immediate investigation and resolution of app performance and server stability issues:** Prioritize addressing the root causes of server problems and order execution delays, as these are directly leading to user losses and dissatisfaction.
*   **Streamline and improve withdrawal processing and payment gateway reliability:** Conduct an urgent review of the withdrawal process and investigate payment gateway failures to ensure timely and successful fund disbursement.
*   **Analyze F&O lot purchase limit feedback and explore potential adjustments:** Conduct a feasibility study to evaluate the impact of current F&O lot purchase limits and explore options for increasing flexibility to address user demand.
