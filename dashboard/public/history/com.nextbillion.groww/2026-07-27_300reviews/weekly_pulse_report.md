## Weekly App Review Pulse: Groww Leadership Briefing
**Reporting Period:** 13 Jul 2026 to 26 Jul 2026

### Executive Summary
App performance issues have significantly impacted user experience this period, driven by UI scaling and feature removal concerns. While "Other" themes remain the most frequent, "App Performance Issues" have emerged as the second most impactful, highlighting an urgent need for attention to fundamental app functionality and user interface design.

### 1. Top Themes & Strategic Insights

*   **App Performance Issues (Impact Score: 161, Reviews: 54):** This theme has surged in importance, indicating a critical need for immediate action. Users are reporting that the app's appearance size is not fitting their mobile screens, hindering their ability to view full details. Furthermore, the removal of previously liked smart features, such as stock growth percentages in watchlists, is leading to user dissatisfaction.
*   **Other (Impact Score: 165, Reviews: 191):** This remains the most frequent theme, suggesting a broad range of user feedback. The top quote highlights a significant concern about the unsolicited promotion of loan services, which users perceive as "irritating" and a potential driver for seeking alternative platforms.
*   **Trading Features (Impact Score: 56, Reviews: 29):** While less impactful than performance issues, feedback here points to specific usability challenges within trading. Users are requesting the default lot size for options to be set to 1, and there's a strong desire for more intuitive ways to set multiple Stop Loss (SL) and Take Profit (TGT) orders simultaneously, drawing comparisons to competitor apps.

### 3. Recommended Actions

*   **Prioritize UI/UX Audit for App Performance:** Immediately investigate and address the app's display issues on various mobile devices to ensure all details are visible and accessible. Simultaneously, review the recent removal of "smart features" to assess user sentiment and consider reintroducing or enhancing them.
*   **Segregate and Refine Cross-Promotion Strategies:** Develop a clear strategy to separate loan-related services from the core stock trading app. This may involve dedicated sections or separate apps entirely to avoid "irritating" users and driving them to competitors.
*   **Enhance Trading Feature Usability:** Expedite the development of a more intuitive interface for setting multiple Stop Loss and Take Profit orders. Explore the feasibility of implementing a drag-and-drop functionality for SL/TGT and ensure the default lot size for options trading is set to 1.
