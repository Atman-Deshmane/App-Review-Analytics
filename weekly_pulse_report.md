## Weekly App Review Pulse: Groww Leadership Briefing
**Reporting Period:** 14 Sep 2026 to 27 Sep 2026

### Executive Summary
This reporting period highlights significant user dissatisfaction stemming from customer support and app performance issues, with these themes dominating negative feedback and impact scores. While trading features and charges/fees are less frequently mentioned, the severity of negative sentiment around app stability and customer service requires immediate attention.

### 1. Top Themes & Strategic Insights

*   **Customer Support (Impact Score: 386, Review Count: 8):** Despite a lower review count, Customer Support generated the highest impact score, indicating severe user frustration. This suggests that when issues arise, the current support system is failing to adequately address them, leading to significant negative sentiment.
*   **App Performance Issues (Impact Score: 196, Review Count: 47):** This theme accounts for a substantial portion of reviews and a considerable impact score. Users are experiencing critical issues like app instability, particularly within live trading sessions and F&O sections, which directly hinders their trading experience.
*   **Other (Impact Score: 249, Review Count: 203):** While "Other" has the highest review count, its impact score is lower than Customer Support and App Performance. This broad category likely includes a wide range of smaller issues, but the sheer volume suggests a need for deeper analysis to identify recurring sub-themes.

### 3. Recommended Actions

*   **Prioritize and Investigate Customer Support Escalations:** Conduct an immediate deep-dive into the customer support process. Identify bottlenecks, response times, and resolution rates for critical issues. Implement a tiered escalation system to ensure severe issues are handled by specialized teams.
*   **Urgent App Performance Audit and Stabilization:** Allocate engineering resources to immediately address the reported app instability and glitches, particularly in live trading sessions and F&O sections. Roll back or thoroughly test any recent layout changes that are negatively impacting trading flow.
*   **Analyze "Other" Theme for Emerging Patterns:** Perform a granular analysis of reviews categorized under "Other" to identify any recurring, low-volume but potentially significant issues that could be precursors to larger problems. This proactive approach can help address issues before they gain more traction.
